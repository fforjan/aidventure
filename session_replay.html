🎮  THE LAST FLIGHT
Session Log
Wake Up
The room is dark. Neon from the sign across the street cuts red stripes through the blinds. The AC rattles. Somewhere below, the city breathes.
3:47 AM.
One message. No sender.
“GET OUT NOW. AIRPORT. FLIGHT 7:15 AM. GATE 12. DO NOT STOP.”
Get the bag and jump through the window.
You’re on the fourth floor. The window stays closed. You take the fire escape instead.
Clean Street
Four flights of cold iron. Diesel, wet stone, something frying nearby. Pre-dawn fog sits low. The airport tower blinks its slow red pulse to the east. Six kilometres.
To the left — market noise, crowds, cover. To the right — the river road, quiet and dark.
Is there any taxi?
The alley is empty. No cars, no cabs — just damp cobblestones and a stray cat.
I go to the market to be lost in the crowd.
Morning Market
Cardamom, diesel fumes, raw fish on ice. Vendors shout across stalls. Good cover.
Then — a figure in black. Moving through the crowd with a stillness that doesn’t belong. No browsing, no hesitation. Every step deliberate. Their eyes find yours across thirty metres and hold. Matching your pace exactly.
Start to run and try to jump into a boat.
The river is three kilometres back. You run — knocking a tower of spice jars across the stone. The vendor screams. The figure in black doesn’t run. They simply disappear.
Police Tangle
Open street. Lungs burning. A police uniform steps directly into your path.
He’s young. Radio crackling. He looks at the chaos behind you, then at you.
“Stop. Identification.”
Run opposite the cop.
💀  DEAD END
Two more uniforms step out from a side street ahead — already coming for the market disturbance. Nowhere to go. A hand closes on your collar. The radio crackles. Someone reads out a description that sounds a lot like you.
The airport tower blinks in the distance. Gate 12. Flight 7:15 AM.
Unreachable.
“Left a trail a mile wide. The city didn’t even have to try.”
